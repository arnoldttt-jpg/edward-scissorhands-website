Slow periods

The usual quiet times of the year for a Melbourne barber, with rough dates. Good moments to switch an offer on.

  1. After New Year2 to 20 JanChristmas cuts are done and many people are away. The quietest stretch of the year.
  2. Late summerMid to late FebBack to school and work; money is tight after the holidays.
  3. EasterWeek of EasterLong weekend and school holidays take people out of town.
  4. Mid-winterMid June to late JulyCold, dark evenings and the July school holidays.
  5. Grand Final weekLast week of SeptFooty holiday and Grand Final weekend.
  6. Melbourne Cup weekFirst week of NovCup Day is a holiday. This one shows in your own figures as the quietest week since October.
  7. Quiet days each weekSun, Mon and TueThe days the fewest barbers are rostered.

These are typical for Melbourne, not yet checked against your own sales history. Only Melbourne Cup week and the quiet weekdays come from your dashboard so far.
