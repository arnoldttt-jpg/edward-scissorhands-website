Brand guidelines

Gold on night.
Since 1991.

The working brand standard for Edward Scissorhands Barber Shop — St Kilda and South Melbourne. Born on Carlisle Street; discreet luxury with a trade heart. Everything here ships on the website, the QR codes, print and signage. If a choice isn't covered, make it feel like the shop at dusk: dark timber, warm metal, one neon sign humming.

01 · The marks

One badge, four cuts. One name, one face.

Clear space around any mark: a quarter of its width on all sides. Never recolour the gold, never outline it, never open the scissor loops or crop the curls. The badge never inverts — its field stays black everywhere.

02 · Colour

Gold is the only accent.

Roughly 80/15/5: near-black grounds, warm off-white text, gold as the single accent. There is no second colour. Gold type under 24px is only ever the condensed label face.

Night#0B0B0C

The page ground. The brand lives on near-black.

Coal#141414

Raised surfaces: cards and panels, one step off night.

Badge field#0C0A08

The warm black inside the badge. Fixed, never inverted.

Bone#F2F0EB

Primary text. A warm off-white, never pure #FFF.

Smoke#A3A09A

Secondary text: subtitles, captions, attributions.

Ash#6F6C66

Muted text and resting links. Decorative below 17px.

Gold#D8B24A

Display accents, rules, icons, large type on night.

Shadow end of the finish; gold on light grounds.

Glint end. Only inside gradients and sheens.

Quiet gold for hovers where full gold would shout.

HairlineWHITE 10%

Every rule and border. 1px, never heavier.

03 · The gold finish

Gold is a material, not a paint.

Flat gold is for rules, labels and icons. Anything larger takes the finish: the 128° gradient below (deep → gold → highlight → gold → deep), or real gold leaf clipped into the letterforms. Never a flat gold rectangle. One gold-leaf moment per screen.

06 · Motifs

The tools are the ornaments.

The tool set

Six trade tools drawn in the same weight: razor, scissors, comb, clippers, hairdryer and spray bottle. Always gold with the shine passing through, one after another. Use them as list markers, perks and section ornaments; never recolour or outline them.

The razor

The brand's bullet point: between marquee words and marking list items, always in the gold finish with its shine sweep.

The hand

Single gold line, drawn by hand. Menu sheets, the QR landing page, closing sections. Enters from the right, rests with a slow sway. Mirrored only for the menu-sheet slide-out.

The diamond

A 7px square turned 45°, stroked in gold, flanked by fading hairlines. The default divider where a section needs ceremony.

The hand, drawn
