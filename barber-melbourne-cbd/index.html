Questions from Melbourne CBD

How long does it take from the CBD?

Around 10 minutes on the route 12 tram from Collins St, or a 25-minute walk from Flinders St or Southern Cross.

Can I book during the work day?

Yes — book online and pick a time that fits around meetings. Lunch slots and after-work times tend to go first.

Should I book or just walk in?

Booking online is the best way in. Our South Melbourne shop runs on bookings from November 2026, and walk-ins are still welcome whenever a chair is free.
