Quick answers.

Can I just walk in?

Yes. Walk-ins are welcome 7 days a week and we'll seat you as soon as a chair is free. Booked appointments go first, so book online for a weekend or a particular barber. Book now

How much is a haircut?

A standard haircut starts from $46. The price depends on the service, the barber and the day, and you see the exact amount before you confirm your booking. See services and prices

What is a fade? What's the difference between a skin fade and a regular fade?

A fade blends the sides and back from short to longer, so there's no hard line. A regular fade stops at a short guard length, usually a No. 1, so there's always a little hair left at the bottom. A skin fade goes right down to bare skin with a shaver, which is the sharpest contrast and shows regrowth sooner, and a zero fade sits in between. We do them all, from low and mid to high, taper, burst and drop. A skin fade is from $57, and you see your barber's exact price before you confirm. See fade haircuts See skin fades

Where are you, and where do I park?

On the corner of Carlisle and Chapel Streets at 190 Carlisle Street, a short walk from Balaclava station on the Sandringham line. There's all-day parking on select streets nearby, and the Route 78 and Carlisle Street trams stop close by.

When are you open?

Seven days a week: Monday to Friday 9am to 7pm, Saturday and Sunday 9am to 5pm. We open most public holidays too, so call the shop if you're planning around one.

Do you cut kids' hair?

Yes, from age 2, first haircuts included. Coming in together? Ask about our Dad & Lad package.
