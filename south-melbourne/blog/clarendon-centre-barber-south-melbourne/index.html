A barber inside the Clarendon Centre

Edward Scissorhands South Melbourne has cut hair in the Clarendon Centre, 261 Clarendon St, since 2015. Who comes in, when to visit and how to get here.

Since 2015, our second shop has been cutting hair a few minutes from the South Melbourne Market. Same standards as Carlisle Street, with a rhythm all its own.

Why a second shop in South Melbourne?

By 2015, Edward Scissorhands had been cutting hair on Carlisle Street in St Kilda for almost twenty-five years. South Melbourne felt like a natural fit for a second shop: a proper village high street, a much-loved market, and a mix of families, office workers and long-time locals. It's the same kind of neighbourhood that has kept Carlisle Street busy since 1991.

We opened inside the Clarendon Centre at 261 Clarendon Street. More than a decade on, the shop has a following of its own, while keeping everything that makes an Edward Scissorhands cut: open seven days, and barbers who take the time to get it right.

“Same standards as Carlisle Street, with a rhythm all its own.Edward Scissorhands, South Melbourne

Who sits in the chair?

Just about everyone. People who live and work around Clarendon Street, families from the surrounding streets, and regulars who've been coming since we opened. They come in for everything from skin fades and tidy tapers to scissor cuts, beard trims and boys' cuts.

If you're short on time, book ahead online and pick a time that suits you.

How do you get to the shop?

You'll find us inside the Clarendon Centre at 261 Clarendon Street, South Melbourne.

New to the area, or just new to us? Book online, or drop in when there's a chair free, and we'll look after you.
