Privacy policy

Last updated 24 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information Edward Scissorhands Barber Shop collects through this website, why, and what we do with it. It covers both shops:

  • St Kilda: Edward Scissorhands Pty Ltd (ABN 37 415 825 760), 190 Carlisle Street, St Kilda VIC 3182.
  • South Melbourne: Barber Brothers Pty Ltd (ABN 33 708 458 049) trading as Edward Scissorhands, The Clarendon Centre, 261 Clarendon Street, South Melbourne VIC 3205.

What we collect

  • Bookings. Online bookings are made through Fresha. When you book, Fresha collects your name, contact details and appointment details, and shares them with us so we can look after your visit. Fresha's own privacy policy applies to the booking: fresha.com/privacy-policy.
  • Job applications. If you apply through our Careers page, we collect what you send us: your name, mobile, email, preferred shop and hours, experience, a link to your work, photos of your work and anything you tell us about yourself.
  • Messages and calls. If you email or phone us, we keep what's needed to reply and help you.
  • Website visits. We use Google Analytics to see how people use the site, such as which pages are visited and roughly where visitors are from. It uses cookies and does not tell us who you are. We also count how often our in-shop QR codes are scanned; this does not identify you.

How we use it

  • To take and manage your bookings, and to contact you about them.
  • To consider job applications and get in touch with applicants.
  • To reply to your messages and calls.
  • To understand how the website is used and make it better.

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't use it for anything you wouldn't reasonably expect.

Who sees it

Only our owners and the staff who need it. We use trusted services to run the business and the website: Fresha (bookings), Google (analytics), Cloudflare (website hosting) and Supabase (secure storage for website forms). Some of these services may store information outside Australia.

How long we keep it

We keep information only as long as we need it. Job applications are kept for up to 3 years in case a role comes up, then deleted, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

Cookies

Google Analytics sets cookies to count visits. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or use Google's opt-out add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout. The site still works without them.

Your choices

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email info@scissorhands.com.au and we'll reply within 30 days. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, tell us first and we'll try to fix it. You can also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
